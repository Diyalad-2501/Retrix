Our Motto

Why Retrix?

E-commerce sellers deal with growing order volumes, rising return rates, and shrinking margins — yet most platforms do not clearly explain why these issues occur. Important signals stay buried, patterns go unnoticed, and decisions are often driven by assumptions instead of clarity.

Retrix focuses on breaking down return behavior across products, categories, and time. By exposing repeat causes, high-risk items, and operational gaps, sellers gain the clarity needed to reduce returns and protect profitability.
